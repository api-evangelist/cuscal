Fraud and scams are rising, and they’re shaking customer confidence at its foundation. Every scam attempt is a moment of vulnerability for your customers. Based on Cuscal’s Quarterly Insights research with over 2,000 Australian consumers, 57% of respondents face scam attempts weekly, and almost one in three has been impacted.
