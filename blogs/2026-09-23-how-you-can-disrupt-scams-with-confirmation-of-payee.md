As real-time payments become the norm, funds can move between accounts in seconds, giving scammers less time to be detected and stopped. This makes preventative controls increasingly important. Confirmation of Payee (CoP) adds an extra layer of protection before a payment is sent, helping customers verify they are paying the intended person or business.
