# Cuscal

> Cuscal Limited (ASX: CCL) is Australia's largest independent provider of payments and regulated data services outside the Big Four banks. Founded in 1966 and an APRA/ASIC-regulated Authorised Deposit-taking Institution, Cuscal supplies the connectivity and processing layer beneath hundreds of banks, mutuals, credit unions, fintechs, non-bank lenders and corporates: scheme card issuing and acquiring, the New Payments Platform (NPP with PayID and PayTo), Direct Entry, BPAY, RTGS, ATM services, AI-driven fraud monitoring, and Consumer Data Right (open banking) data-holder and accredited-data-recipient services. Its API posture is B2B and relationship-gated: a public Developer Hub documents CMS REST APIs for card issuing, but full reference and machine-readable specs sit behind client onboarding.

## APIs
- [Cuscal Digital Card Issuance API](https://www.cuscal.com/payments/issuing/digital-enablement/): Instant digital card issuance and provisioning of cards into mobile/digital wallets ("the Pays"), with webhook notifications for card lifecycle events.
- [Cuscal Card Controls API](https://www.cuscal.com/payments/issuing/digital-enablement/): Lock/unlock cards and block transaction types so cardholders and back-office staff can control card usage and reduce fraud.
- [Cuscal PIN Services API](https://www.cuscal.com/payments/issuing/digital-enablement/): Self-service PIN set and change functionality that issuers embed in mobile payment apps.

## Event surface
- Webhooks: Cuscal's CMS REST APIs deliver webhook notifications for card lifecycle, transaction-status, and account events (schemas gated behind onboarding). No public AsyncAPI is published.

## Notes
- No public, downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger definition is available; the reference and specs are gated behind client onboarding.
- Authentication scheme is not publicly documented; consistent with onboarded API-key / OAuth2 client-credentials for issuer integrations, but unverified.
- Standards participation: Consumer Data Right (CDR), NPP/ISO 20022 (PayID, PayTo), BPAY, Direct Entry (BECS), RTGS, and the Visa/Mastercard/eftpos card schemes.
